Three risk profiles — same framework, different target mixes of stable vs. speculative holdings:

  • Conservative — mostly Foundation (stable, 60–80% target); small Opportunity slice; minimal Exploration.
  • Balanced — even spread across Foundation (40–60%), Opportunity (20–40%), and Exploration (10–20%).
  • Aggressive — lighter Foundation (20–40%); heavier Opportunity and Exploration (each 20–40%).

These profiles differ in composition, not a promised return ranking. HT-B backtest windows (below) show similar mean CAGRs across profiles — none has demonstrated a reliably superior live pattern so far. Pick a profile for how much stable vs. speculative exposure you want, not because one is “the winner.”
