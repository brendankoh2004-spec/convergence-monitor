Observe. Record. Track. Repeat.

01 · Observe

At each review

Convergence observes a company’s price and the books using only information available at that point in time.

02 · Record

The state

Those observations form the company’s current state — a description of how price and the books relate at that moment.

03 · Track

The change

At the next review, the state may remain the same or change. Convergence records that change rather than treating every observation as an isolated snapshot.

04 · Repeat

Over time

The process continues over time, creating a record of how states change.

Why track change?

A current state tells us where a company is. A change tells us that something happened. We track when the observed state changes — not because change is good or bad, but because change is information worth recording.

Some changes may historically lead toward states worth understanding better. Others may not. Convergence does not assume which is which — it records the change first, then tests what historically followed.

What does “unusual” mean? “Unusual” does not mean close to a 52-week high or low. It refers to how current price behavior compares with the company’s own longer-term history.
Technical detail

Readings reflect averaged year-over-year momentum compared to a rolling multi-year window, normalized against the company’s own history — not a generic industry average or a 1-year chart position.

View methodology →

Part 1 uses genuine state changes only (no significance, duration, or desirability filter) across 341 companies, 2017–2023. Monthly observation recorded 10,140 genuine state changes vs 10,408 at weekly (97.4% of weekly volume; 2.64% fewer).

Part 2 pools 10,206 genuine monthly transitions on the same panel. Pearson χ² test of independence on the 9×9 origin→destination matrix (χ² = 35,232.09, df = 64, p < 0.001); Cramér’s V = 0.657. Top-10 recurring joint paths: 73.95% of all transitions; 12 paths with ≥100 occurrences. Permutation corroboration: observed χ² exceeds 99.9th percentile of 10,000 label-shuffle nulls.

Part 3 uses pre-registered historical comparison units (330 comparisons; yes-rate 50.61%, rounded to 51% for display). Reported rates are structural pattern detection, not returns or investment performance.
